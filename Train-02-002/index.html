WHAT IS BOOTHER DISEASE ?
• It is an inflammation and injury of the joints. • It is a condition where the function of joints, ligaments, muscles, nerves, tendons and spine is impaired.
WHY OSTEOARTHRITIS ?
• The weather changes suddenly. • Excessive exercise. • Hard labor. • Working in the wrong position.
SYMPTOMS OF OSTEOARTHRITIS ?
• Pain, swelling, stiffness. • Reduced mobility.
PEOPLE WHO OFTEN GET SICK ?
The subjects most often suffer from the disease: • Old person. • Obese people. • People with a genetic history. • People with joint injuries.
